Children's Health Ireland • Dublin, Ireland • Research Presentation

Developing Immersive Virtual Reality Experiences to Support Children Across the Hospital Journey and Clinical Procedures

Presented work on the development of immersive Virtual Reality experiences designed to support children throughout hospital journeys and clinical procedures. The project combines immersive familiarisation experiences with interactive coping supports and follows an implementation-informed and user-centred design framework involving children, caregivers and multidisciplinary clinical teams.
